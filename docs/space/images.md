# Image research for chapter 1

Picture research for `src/app/space/chapter1.ts` (32 beats). Compiled 18 Oct 2026. Licences were read from the Wikimedia Commons file pages (licence tag, author and source), not from search snippets. Direct URLs are the Commons 'original' files; no files were downloaded. 'Doubt' notes flag weak provenance. TIFF originals need a thumbnail or conversion for the web.

Beats follow story order and use story ids (research brief 07b is `ferdinand-1`, 18b is `black-brant`). TIROS and Echo also have NASA-site originals named in the notes.
