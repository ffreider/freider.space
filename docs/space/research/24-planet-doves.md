# 24 · Planet's Doves and the small-satellite flood (2013–2019)

## 1. What happened
- **First Doves (2013):** Dove 2 launched on 19 April 2013 on a Soyuz from Baikonur, riding with Bion-M1. Dove 1 launched on 21 April 2013 on the first Antares from Wallops and re-entered on 27 April. Dove 3 and Dove 4 followed on a Dnepr from Dombarovsky on 21 November 2013. All were 3U CubeSats (about 10 × 10 × 30 cm) built largely from off-the-shelf parts.
- **Flock 1 (2014):** 28 Doves launched on 9 January 2014 aboard Cygnus Orb-1 to the ISS. From **11 February 2014** they were pushed out of the Japanese Kibo module by NanoRacks deployers. At the time, this was the largest Earth-imaging constellation ever put in orbit.
- **The big jump (2017):** India's PSLV-C37 carried **88 Flock-3p Doves** among 104 satellites on 15 February 2017. The SATCAT lists 106 catalogued objects for that launch, 88 of them FLOCK.
- **Mission 1:** On 9 November 2017, co-founder Will Marshall announced that Planet could image the entire Earth's landmass every day. By then the company had launched over 300 satellites and was operating about 200.
- **The wider trend (local SATCAT, payloads catalogued per year):**

## 2. People
- **Will Marshall, Robbie Schingler and Chris Boshuizen** were former NASA Ames engineers who founded the company as Cosmogia in 2010. It was renamed Planet Labs, then Planet.
- **Koichi Wakata** (JAXA), on Expedition 38, deployed and photographed the first Flock 1 pair from the station.
- **NanoRacks** supplied the ISS CubeSat deployers.

## 3. Why it mattered on the ground
- A fresh picture of almost anywhere on land, every day, changed what counts as evidence. Researchers, aid groups and journalists can watch farms, forests, floods and construction day by day instead of waiting weeks for a single expensive image.
- In September 2018, NASA bought Planet data (with DigitalGlobe and Spire) in a pilot to test commercial small-sat data for science. NASA's report found the data useful but the licences restrictive.
- The flip side: many short-lived cubesats meant many more catalogued objects. The local SATCAT has 657 Dove/Flock entries, of which only 114 are still in orbit.

## 6. Commonly misstated
- **"Imaging the whole Earth daily":** Planet's own claim is about the **landmass**, not the oceans, and at medium resolution (a few metres per pixel). It is not a sharp daily picture of everything. I did not verify Planet's exact resolution figure in this session.
- **Launch dates of ISS-deployed Doves:** The SATCAT records Flock 1 and other ISS-deployed cubesats with the ISS's own international designator and launch date (**1998-067, 20 Nov 1998**). Don't quote these as launch dates.
- **"First Dove = Flock 1":** Four Doves flew in 2013 before Flock 1.
- **"104 Planet satellites on PSLV-C37":** The launch carried 104 satellites, of which 88 were Planet Doves.
